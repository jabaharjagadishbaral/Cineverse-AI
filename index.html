CineVerse AI

A hybrid deep-learning recommender. It learns from what people watched, reads what each film is about, and corrects for the bias toward blockbusters. Move the controls and watch the list change.

Cold-start

New viewers have no history, so the model drops its ID embeddings and ranks from genre taste. New films get a small boost until they gather ratings.

Sparse data

Few ratings means little collaborative signal. The blend weight grows with history, so sparse users lean on content instead of noise.

Popularity bias

Hits are down-weighted in training and in ranking, then a diversity pass stops the list from repeating one genre.

This page runs a lightweight JavaScript port of the scoring logic. The trainable PyTorch model and FastAPI service are in the backend folder.
